Senior Backend Engineer — Payments

AtlasPay · Remote (US/CA) · Full-time

$155k–$180k Equity + Bonus Hiring now

Role overview

Own the core payments platform handling 10M+ transactions monthly. You’ll architect services, improve performance, and mentor a team of backend engineers.

What you’ll do

  • Design resilient payment services with clear SLAs.
  • Partner with product to ship new monetization features.
  • Improve observability, alerting, and reliability metrics.
  • Guide architecture decisions for scaling across regions.

Requirements

  • 5+ years backend experience in production systems.
  • Strong Node.js + Postgres expertise (Go a plus).
  • Experience with event-driven architecture and Kafka.
  • Comfortable working with PCI and financial compliance.

Benefits

  • Health, dental, and vision for you + dependents.
  • $2,000 annual learning stipend.

Team size: 8 engineers, 2 product managers

Interview loop: Recruiter screen → Technical → System design → Team chat
